Goa airport luxury car rental

Goa has two airports that matter for a rental handover. Manohar International Airport at Mopa is the main one for most domestic and international arrivals. Dabolim still handles flights and is the closer airport if you are sleeping in the south.

Send the flight number when you book. The car is either waiting with a chauffeur or handed to you at an agreed point once you have cleared arrivals. Do not plan a one-day self-drive around a late landing. The minimum remains two days.

If the flight lands after dark and the hotel is in Palolem, take a chauffeur for the first night even if the rest of the stay is self-drive. The road south is simple in daylight and tedious with luggage at midnight.

Documents for a self-drive handover are a driving licence valid for the car and a government photo ID. International guests should keep their passport with them at pickup.
