Good to know

Questions, answered

Do you offer self-drive luxury cars in Goa?

Yes. The whole fleet can be booked self-drive. Four-wheelers need a minimum of two days. One-day self-drive rentals are not available.

Are chauffeur-driven luxury cars available?

Yes. A chauffeur is ₹3,500 for 8 hours and can be added to any car. It is the usual choice for airport days, weddings and full-day sightseeing.

What documents are required to rent a luxury car?

For self-drive, bring a valid driving licence and a government photo ID. The security deposit is confirmed on WhatsApp before handover. International guests should also carry a passport.

Is airport delivery available in Goa?

Yes. Cars are delivered to Manohar International Airport (Mopa), Dabolim, hotels, villas and private addresses in North and South Goa. Send the flight number with the booking.

What types of luxury cars are available?

Convertibles and sports cars (Mustang GT 500, Mini Cooper, Mercedes C300, Audi A3, BMW Z4), luxury SUVs (Defender 110, BMW X5) and an executive sedan (BMW 5 Series).
